8. Friends' rooms

If you use Friends' rooms, you get a friend code. Anyone you give it to, or whose code you enter, becomes your friend, and you can see each other's rooms (the room on your home, your friend, its growth stage, the name you show, and hearts received) and give each other a heart once a day. Your fridge, shopping, diet, allergies, and coins are never shown. Only share your code with people you want to see your room. You can remove a friend at any time. Hearts have no value and cannot be exchanged for anything.

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or for abusing other users. Our server sets the name your friends see and refuses names with profanity, slurs, hate or sexual terms, or that pose as our staff. You can report or block a friend from their room. Blocking removes them as a friend, and they can't add you again with your friend code. We review reports and may hide a name or remove a friendship that breaks Section 3, remove the user from Friends' rooms, or suspend their account under Section 15. A name reported by three different friends is hidden even before we review it. Our Privacy Policy explains what a report contains.
