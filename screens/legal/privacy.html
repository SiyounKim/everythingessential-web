Friddy is an AI-powered kitchen, grocery, and food-planning application operated by Everything Essential. Friddy helps users manage kitchen inventory, track expiration dates, generate recipe and shopping recommendations, compare grocery options, analyze receipts, and improve grocery spending decisions.

By accessing or using Friddy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, personalize, secure, and improve Friddy.

1.1 Account information

When you create or use a Friddy account, we may collect:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Login and authentication information
  • Profile preferences, such as language, household size, budget range, dietary preferences, allergy information, and grocery preferences
  • Subscription or premium status

Guest (anonymous) accounts. When you first open Friddy, the app signs you in automatically with an anonymous guest account so you can use Friddy without registering or providing any personal details. A guest account is identified only by a random account ID issued by Firebase Authentication; for a guest account we do not collect your name, email, or phone number. Data you create as a guest — such as kitchen inventory, shopping lists, and preferences — is stored under that anonymous ID.

Upgrading a guest account. You can later create a permanent account by adding an email address and password. Your existing guest account is then upgraded in place: the same account ID is kept, so the data you created as a guest carries over to your registered account. From that point we collect and store your email address (and display name, if provided). If you instead sign in to a different existing account, the data under your guest account is not transferred and remains associated only with the anonymous ID. You can delete a guest or registered account at any time in the app (Profile → Settings → Account), which removes the associated data on the same schedule described in "Data retention".

1.2 Kitchen and inventory data

To provide Friddy's inventory and food-planning features, we may collect information you enter or generate in the app, including:

  • Food items in your kitchen inventory
  • Quantity, unit, category, and storage location
  • Expiration dates or estimated shelf life
  • Food status, such as fresh, expiring soon, expired, or consumed
  • Reorder preferences
  • Notes, tags, or custom item details

1.3 Grocery, shopping, and budget data

To support grocery planning, price comparison, and smart basket optimization, we may collect:

  • Shopping list items
  • Grocery budget preferences
  • Preferred grocery stores or platforms
  • Purchase history entered or confirmed by you
  • Estimated prices, selected offers, and basket recommendations
  • Store ratings or feedback submitted by you
  • Aggregated price history per item (your past purchase prices), used to detect savings opportunities
  • Savings records (such as avoided duplicate purchases or purchases below your average price) displayed in the savings dashboard
  • The stores and deals you apply to your shopping list in Flyer Deals (Canada and the US): store name, sale and regular price, valid-until date, distance, and the price and store before the deal (so you can revert it)

1.4 Receipt and fridge/kitchen photo data

If you use receipt scanning, fridge/kitchen photo scanning, or purchase verification features, we may collect:

  • Photos or images of receipts that you upload
  • Photos of your refrigerator, pantry, or kitchen that you upload so Friddy can identify food items (these photos may incidentally capture other items visible in the frame)
  • Receipt text extracted from the image
  • Food item names, estimated quantity, and category detected from fridge/kitchen photos
  • Purchased item names, quantities, prices, store names, purchase dates, and totals
  • Matching results between your receipt and shopping list
  • Information needed to update your inventory after a purchase or a fridge/kitchen scan
You should avoid uploading receipts or fridge/kitchen photos that contain unnecessary sensitive information. If possible, crop or hide information not needed for purchase verification or food-item recognition.

1.5 AI interaction data

Friddy uses Anthropic's Claude API for recipe link analysis, expiry prediction, price insights, and AI insights, and Google's Gemini API for receipt and fridge/kitchen photo analysis, for extracting food items from voice-input transcripts, and for Flyer Deals' lowest-price search. Both providers contractually agree not to use data submitted through their APIs to train their models. Data sent to these providers is processed only to generate the specific output requested by the user and is not retained by the providers beyond their stated retention periods.

Depending on the AI feature, we may process:

  • Your inventory data
  • Dietary preferences and allergy settings
  • Shopping list data
  • Receipt-related data
  • Fridge/kitchen photo data
  • Voice-input transcripts (converted text)
  • Budget preferences
  • Recipe links you paste and the public content of those web pages
  • Shopping list item names and your area (city name and coordinates) for Flyer Deals' search
  • User prompts or feedback given to AI features
  • AI-generated recommendations and outputs

When you search for the lowest prices in Flyer Deals (Canada and the US), the names of the items on your shopping list (up to 30) and the area you set (city name and coordinates) are sent to Friddy's server (Google Cloud Functions, us-central1, United States), which sends them to Google Gemini (with Google Search) to find this week's prices at nearby stores. The results (stores, prices, and the item names searched) are kept on our servers by area (about 5 km cells) without your account ID, reused for searches in the same area for 24 hours, and replaced by the next search.

1.6 Location data

Friddy uses the area you set to compare nearby stores' flyers and prices, recommend groceries, show regional prices, and offer market-specific features. We collect:

  • The country you live in, and your city or neighborhood name
  • Your area's coordinates (latitude and longitude): when you choose an area from the list or by search, its representative coordinates; when you tap "Use current location", the location your device gives at that moment (accurate to about 100 m), saved to your profile
  • Store availability near your selected area

Friddy asks for location permission only when you tap "Use current location", and reads your location once at that moment. Friddy does not track your location continuously or in the background. You can change your area in your Profile at any time.

1.7 Usage, device, and technical data

We may automatically collect technical and usage information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data
  • Feature usage events
  • Performance data
  • Authentication and security logs
  • Approximate session activity

This information helps us maintain app stability, detect abuse, improve user experience, and secure the Service. Device integrity signals (Google Play Integrity / Apple App Attest) may be processed to prevent abuse.

Advertising ID (Android)

  • Google Firebase Analytics: usage statistics
  • Google AdMob: serving and measuring ads, preventing invalid ad traffic, and personalized ads (see Section 5, "Advertising (Google AdMob) and your choices")
  • On iOS, Friddy does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected.
  • You can delete your advertising ID (so it is not used for personalized ads) or reset it (which gives you a new one) in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads on some devices).

1.8 Voice input data

If you use the feature that adds items to your shopping list by voice, we collect and process:

  • The converted text (transcript) of what you say in order to add items to your shopping list
  • The food items extracted from that text (name, quantity, unit, and category)
We do not collect voice (audio) data. Friddy receives only the converted text.

The process works as follows:

  1. You tap the microphone button in the app and say what you need to buy.
  2. Your device's operating system converts the speech to text using its own speech recognition (iOS: Apple Speech Recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer); Android: Google Speech Recognition (SpeechRecognizer)). If your device does not have an offline recognition model for that language, the operating system may send the voice data to Apple's or Google's servers to perform the conversion. This is behavior of the device's operating system and is not controlled by Friddy.
  3. Only the converted text (transcript) is sent to Friddy's server (Google Cloud Functions). App versions 4.2.1 and later use the Seoul, South Korea (asia-northeast3) region; version 4.2.0 uses the United States (us-central1) region. The voice (audio) data itself is not sent to Friddy's server, and Friddy does not collect or store it.
  4. Friddy's server passes the transcript to Google Gemini (United States) to extract food items (name, quantity, unit, and category).
  5. The extracted results are shown in the app, and only the items you review and select are saved to your shopping list (covered by the grocery, shopping, and budget data described in Section 1.3).

The transcript is used solely to extract items; it is not stored in our database and is discarded as soon as processing completes. Our server operation logs do not record the content of the transcript — only a user identifier, the character count, and the language code. However, if item extraction fails, part of the AI response (up to 500 characters) may remain in the operation logs for troubleshooting, and this may include recognized food names.

Each recording is limited to a maximum of 3 minutes, and each transcript to a maximum of 1,500 characters.

1.9 Friddy Room data

  • Coin balance (bought and earned coins kept apart) and coin history (source, amount, balance, date and time, and the mission, item or coin pack involved)
  • Today's missions completed and rewards claimed, whether the all-clear bonus was paid, and today's ad rewards (up to 3 a day)
  • The IDs of recipes and the fingerprints of receipts already rewarded (a receipt's fingerprint is a one-way value made from the store, total and items), so the same recipe or receipt is not rewarded twice
  • Visit record: last visit date, current, longest and total days visited (recorded by date)
  • The friends, items, room themes, decor and rooms you own, what you use in each room and which room home shows, and the friend you picked first
  • The last day you opened the app, and whether you came back that day after days away (used only to choose the friend's face)
  • Saved recipes (up to 200 recipe IDs; for the content of recipes saved from a link, see Section 1.10)
  • When the room was last saved (for syncing between devices)
This contains nothing you write yourself; it is created automatically as you use the app. It is created the first time you open the app, for every account including guest accounts, and each day you open the app is recorded.

1.10 Recipes you save from a link (only if you save one)

  • For each recipe that Recipe finder analyzed from a link and that you chose to save: the link, title, servings, ingredients (name, amount and unit), steps, and when you saved it

We store the link you entered; if it has extra information after "?", such as search terms or a share code, that is stored too. A desktop Naver Blog link is stored as the mobile link to the same post that the analysis uses instead. Links you analyze but do not save are not stored.

We do not store whether you have each ingredient; the app compares the recipe with your current inventory each time you open it. We do not use this information for advertising or include it in ad requests, and we do not give it to anyone other than Google LLC (Firebase), which stores it for us.

1.11 Coin purchases (only if you buy coins)

  • The store (App Store or Google Play), the product, the number of coins, the store's transaction number, whether it was a real or test purchase, whether and when the coins were paid or refunded, and your account identifier

Payment is handled by the store; we do not receive your card number or other payment details. When you buy, the app sends the store a one-way value made from your account identifier, so the coins go only to the account that bought them.

1.12 Friends' rooms (only if you use them)

  • What your friends see: the room on your home (your friend's kind, what it wears, the room theme and decor), your friend's growth stage, the name you show friends (your profile name unless you change it), the hearts your room received, and your friend code
  • Your friend list (your friends' account identifiers, up to 50) and the last day you gave each friend a heart

This is created the first time you open Friends' rooms. Only people you became friends with by a friend code can see it; your fridge, inventory, shopping, diet, allergies and coins are never shown. Removing a friend removes you from each other's lists and deletes the heart dates; deleting your account deletes all of it and removes you from your friends' lists.

Information that Google LLC collects directly through its SDK in the app for advertising is described in Section 5, "Advertising (Google AdMob) and your choices". We do not receive or keep the information Google collects; we keep only the coins paid for ads you watched and today's ad-reward count, as part of the Friddy Room data above.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes.

2.1 To provide core app features

We use your data to create and manage your account, store and display your kitchen inventory, track expiration dates, generate recipe suggestions, build shopping lists, recommend grocery purchases, analyze receipts and fridge/kitchen photos to automatically recognize food items and help populate your inventory, update your inventory after confirmed purchases, and provide spending and waste insights.

2.2 To personalize recommendations

We may use your information to personalize recipe recommendations, expiring-food alerts, shopping suggestions, budget-aware grocery plans, store or product recommendations, smart basket optimization results, and reorder reminders.

2.3 To provide AI-powered features

We may use your data to generate AI-assisted outputs, including meal ideas based on inventory, estimated expiration dates and shelf-life predictions, grocery optimization suggestions, price insights based on your own purchase history and current market prices, receipt review results, automatic food-item recognition from fridge/kitchen photos, budget-saving recommendations, recipe extraction from recipe and cooking video links you provide, food waste reduction insights, savings tracking showing how much Friddy helped you save, personalized shopping plans, and this week's lowest prices at nearby stores in Flyer Deals (Canada and the US).

AI-generated results may be inaccurate or incomplete. You should review recommendations before relying on them, especially for allergies, dietary restrictions, prices, or purchase decisions.

2.4 To process paid features and coin purchases

If Friddy sells paid features or coins, we use account, subscription status and purchase records to confirm payments, enable paid features, pay the coins you bought, prevent unauthorized access, and manage billing-related access through the app stores. Friddy does not collect your card number or other payment details; payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play.

2.5 To improve and secure the Service

We may use information to debug errors and crashes, improve app performance, prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access, enforce our terms and policies, monitor system reliability, and test and improve product features.

2.6 To communicate with you

We may use your contact information to send account-related notices, security alerts, service updates, customer support responses, important policy updates, and optional product or feature announcements, where permitted.

2.7 To provide the Friddy Room, ads, and Friends' rooms

  • Providing the Friddy Room: earning and spending coins, paying mission and visit rewards, decorating your friend and room, saving recipes, applying daily reward limits, and restoring your room after a reinstall or on a new phone
  • Providing rewarded ads: giving you coins for ads you choose to watch (10 coins per ad, up to 3 a day)
  • Selling coins: confirming payments and paying the coins, making sure a payment is paid once and only to the account that made it, taking coins back when a payment is refunded, and answering questions about purchases
  • Keeping recipes you save from a link: showing them again in Saved recipes, comparing them with your current inventory to show what is missing, and adding missing ingredients to your shopping list
  • Providing Friends' rooms: issuing friend codes, adding and removing friends, showing your room to your friends, and giving a heart once a day
  • Providing Flyer Deals: finding this week's prices at stores near the area you set and comparing them with your shopping list

3.2 With AI providers

When you use AI-powered features, relevant data may be sent to third-party AI providers to generate outputs. We send only the data needed for the feature. For example, the receipt-review feature may send receipt text or image information to Google Gemini to identify purchased items. The fridge/kitchen scan feature uploads your photo to Firebase Storage and sends it to Google Gemini to identify the food items in the image (name, quantity, and category); you review the detected items before they are added to your inventory. When you add shopping-list items by voice, only the transcript converted on your device is sent to Friddy's server and then to Google Gemini to extract food items; the voice (audio) data itself is never sent to Friddy's server (see Section 1.8). When you paste a recipe web page or YouTube cooking video link, Friddy's server retrieves the page's public content (for a YouTube video, its public title and description through the YouTube Data API) and sends it to Anthropic Claude to extract ingredients and cooking steps. Friddy does not download, store, or process the video content itself, and keeps an analyzed recipe only if you tap "Save" (see Section 1.10). Flyer Deals sends your shopping list item names and your area to Google Gemini to find nearby prices (see Section 1.5). On the shopping screen, when you search for an item, Friddy uses the Coupang Partners API to show matching product search results and recommendations, including current prices and affiliate purchase links. Only the item name you search is sent as a search query; no personal data is shared with Coupang through this search. Friddy participates in the Coupang Partners program and may earn a commission from qualifying purchases made through these links.

3.3 With affiliate or grocery partners

Friddy displays product search results, prices, and purchase links from grocery partners such as Coupang on the shopping screen, retrieved via the Coupang Partners API. Friddy may also include affiliate links on the Everything Essential website and blog. If you click a purchase link in the app or on our website:

  • The partner may collect information under its own privacy policy
  • Friddy may receive limited affiliate attribution information
  • Friddy may track whether a link was clicked for analytics or commission purposes
  • Friddy does not control the partner's privacy practices

Unless clearly stated, Friddy does not automatically complete purchases on your behalf.

3.4 For legal, safety, or compliance reasons

We may disclose information if we believe it is necessary to comply with applicable law or legal process, respond to lawful requests from authorities, protect the rights, property, or safety of Everything Essential, users, or others, investigate fraud, security incidents, or misuse, or enforce our agreements or policies.

3.5 Business transfers

If Everything Essential is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will take reasonable steps to ensure continued protection of user information.

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android), App set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device
  • IP address (used to estimate your approximate location)
  • Device information (such as device model, operating system and version, language settings and app information)

  • On Android, in Korea, the United States, Canada and elsewhere, ads may be personalized without a separate consent step; you can opt out as described in "Your choices".
  • On iOS, Friddy does not ask for tracking permission and requests only non-personalized ads.

6. How long: We do not receive or keep the information Google collects. Google keeps it according to its own privacy policy.

  • We do keep the coins paid for ads you watched (amount, date and time) and how many ad rewards you got today, as part of your Friddy Room data (see Section 1.9), and ad impressions and rewards may be recorded in our usage statistics (Firebase Analytics; see Section 6, "Automatic collection technologies").
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Watching ads is optional. No ad plays unless you tap an ad button. Opening the Missions screen may still request an ad in advance, and the consent check when the app starts happens either way.

8. If you opt out: on Android, after you delete your advertising ID you may see ads that are not personalized. Every feature of the app works without watching ads, and you can still earn coins through missions and visits.

9. We do not use the diet, allergy or other information you enter in Friddy for advertising, and we do not include it in ad requests to Google. We limit ads to Google's "PG" (parental guidance) content rating or lower. Friddy is not directed at children under 14.

10. Questions about advertising data: our privacy officer (Siyoun Kim, ee.friddy@gmail.com).

1.8 Voice input data

If you use the feature that adds items to your shopping list by voice, we collect and process:

  • The converted text (transcript) of what you say in order to add items to your shopping list
  • The food items extracted from that text (name, quantity, unit, and category)
We do not collect voice (audio) data. Friddy receives only the converted text.

The process works as follows:

  1. You tap the microphone button in the app and say what you need to buy.
  2. Your device's operating system converts the speech to text using its own speech recognition (iOS: Apple Speech Recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer); Android: Google Speech Recognition (SpeechRecognizer)). If your device does not have an offline recognition model for that language, the operating system may send the voice data to Apple's or Google's servers to perform the conversion. This is behavior of the device's operating system and is not controlled by Friddy.
  3. Only the converted text (transcript) is sent to Friddy's server (Google Cloud Functions, us-central1, United States). The voice (audio) data itself is not sent to Friddy's server, and Friddy does not collect or store it.
  4. Friddy's server passes the transcript to Google Gemini (United States) to extract food items (name, quantity, unit, and category).
  5. The extracted results are shown in the app, and only the items you review and select are saved to your shopping list (covered by the grocery, shopping, and budget data described in Section 1.3).

The transcript is used solely to extract items; it is not stored in our database and is discarded as soon as processing completes. Our server operation logs do not record the content of the transcript — only a user identifier, the character count, and the language code. However, if item extraction fails, part of the AI response (up to 500 characters) may remain in the operation logs for troubleshooting, and this may include recognized food names.

Each recording is limited to a maximum of 3 minutes, and each transcript to a maximum of 1,500 characters.
